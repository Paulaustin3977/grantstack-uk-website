The live UK home-energy grant landscape

Six schemes change per year. Three of them changed in 2026 alone. Most guides published before January 2026 are out of date on at least two of them.

Largest single grant

Boiler Upgrade Scheme

Deducted from your installer's invoice. £7,500 standard; £9,000 off-gas until 31 Mar 2027. The installer applies; you just consent.

  • Air source heat pump — £7,500 / £9,000
  • Ground source heat pump — £7,500 / £9,000
  • Biomass boiler — £5,000
  • Stackable with Warm Homes Loan Scheme
Free upgrades

Warm Homes: Local Grant

Means-tested (income ≤ £36k or qualifying benefit) free insulation, heat pump, solar or battery installed by your local council.

  • EPC D-G required
  • Property in England
  • Year 2 (2026/27) oversubscribed in many councils
  • Register for Year 3 from 1 April 2027
New for 2026

Warm Homes Loan Scheme

Government-supported reduced-rate consumer loan for solar, battery, heat pumps — lender-led, launched September 2026.

  • Up to 20% capital grant to the lender
  • Up to 5 percentage points off the rate
  • 3-10 year loans only
  • Heat pump loan cap £20k air, £35k ground
Non-repayable

Disabled Facilities Grant

Up to £30,000 (England) or £36,000 (Wales) for essential home adaptations. Means-tested for adults, NOT means-tested for under-19s.

  • Wet rooms, ramps, stairlifts
  • OT assessment is the gateway
  • Stackable with BUS for separate measures
  • £723m national budget for 2026/27
If you cannot afford essentials

Crisis and Resilience Fund

Replaced the Household Support Fund on 1 April 2026, running to 31 March 2029. You do not need to be on benefits to apply.

  • Administered by your local council
  • For financial shocks & essentials
  • Non-repayable grants
  • Apply at gov.uk/cost-living-help-local-council
Until 31 Mar 2027

0% VAT on energy-saving materials

Zero-rated VAT on heat pumps, biomass boilers, solar, battery and qualifying insulation. Saves 20% on the parts and 5% was reinstated at 5% — the 0% window closes 31 March 2027.

  • Ask for VAT-exclusive quotes
  • Apply via your installer
  • Stackable with all schemes above
  • No application needed

Who this is for

UK homeowners off the gas grid

You heat with oil or LPG. The £9,000 BUS uplift closes 31 March 2027. The playbook walks you through the four-month decision window in order: BUS first, Warm Homes Local Grant second, Warm Homes Loan Scheme third.

UK homeowners on a low income

You have an EPC of D, E, F or G. Apply for the Warm Homes Local Grant first — it's a free, council-arranged install. While you wait, use the playbook to plan ahead.

UK homeowners planning a heat pump in 2026/27

Landlords with EPC upgrade obligations

UK families with a disabled household member

Can I apply for a BUS grant myself?
No. Only an MCS-certified installer can apply. You consent via a secure Ofgem link. Chapter 3 of the playbook walks through the four steps.
What if I'm on benefits — do I still get the £7,500 BUS?
Yes. BUS is not means-tested. You can combine it with means-tested schemes (Warm Homes Local Grant, DFG) for other measures.
