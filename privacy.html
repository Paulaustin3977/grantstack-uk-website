Privacy notice

Effective 16 October 2026. Plain English. We try to collect the least data we can.

Who we are

GrantStack UK is an independent UK consumer publisher of the UK Home Energy & Grants Stacking Playbook. We are not affiliated with any installer, lender, council, government department or marketing network. We do not sell personal data. We do not run advertising networks on this site.

What we collect

  • When you buy the playbook: name, email, payment confirmation reference. Payment card details are handled by Stripe and never touch our servers.
  • When you email us: whatever you tell us, kept only as long as needed to respond.
  • When you use this website: anonymous page-view counts (see "Analytics" below).

What we never collect

  • No fingerprinting, no advertising IDs.
  • No tracking pixels from Meta, Google Ads, TikTok or any similar network.
  • No scripts that follow you off this site.

Analytics

The free Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon is enabled on this site (no cookies, no fingerprinting, no personal data shared with Cloudflare beyond IP — Cloudflare deletes the IP after 24h). We can also disable it on request. Email hello@grantstack.uk.

How long we keep your data

  • Order details (name, email, payment confirmation reference): 6 years for HMRC compliance.
  • Email replies: deleted within 12 months unless you ask us to keep them.
  • Anonymous analytics: aggregated monthly and dropped after 24 months.

Your rights under UK GDPR

You can ask us at any time to confirm what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for marketing. Email hello@grantstack.uk. We will respond within one calendar month, as required by the UK GDPR.
