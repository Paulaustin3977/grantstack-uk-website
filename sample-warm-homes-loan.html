Chapter 5 — Warm Homes Loan Scheme

The new consumer loan product launched September 2026 with up to 20% principal-reducing capital grant paid to the lender, generating up to 5 percentage points off the borrower's nominal rate. Eligible measures, lender eligibility, current status, and the stacking recipe with BUS.

Action summary

If your household income is too high for Warm Homes: Local Grant but you cannot afford £12,000-£30,000 upfront for a heat pump, the Warm Homes Loan Scheme is your fallback. It launched to consumers in September 2026. Not every lender offers it on day one.

What it is

A reduced-rate consumer loan offered by FCA-authorised participating lenders. The government pays a non-repayable capital grant of up to 20% of the loan amount directly to the lender, which lowers the rate by up to 5 percentage points. You borrow at a lower rate than an unsecured personal loan.

What it is not

  • It is not a free grant.
  • It is not a government loan (the government does not lend).
  • It is not available from every lender (only those approved by DESNZ in onboarding windows).

Eligible measures and loan caps

MeasureLoan cap
Solar PV£15,000

Heat network connection£7,500
Micro-renewables (wind, hydro)£20,000

Borrower eligibility

  • Owner-occupier or private-rented-sector landlord.
  • One loan per property (multi-property owners can take multiple loans).
  • Loan must be for an eligible measure installed by an MCS-certified contractor.
  • Subject to the lender's standard credit, affordability and KYC checks.
  • Loan term: 3-10 years.

Current lender status (October 2026)

  • Phase 1 lender application window closed 5pm, 17 July 2026.
  • Loans to the public targeted for September 2026 (now live for some lenders).
  • Phase 2 lender onboarding window expected late 2026 for products going live early 2027.
  • The Scheme Rules require DESNZ to publish a participating-lender list — check the gov.uk scheme page before applying.

How to apply

  1. Use a participating lender from the official DESNZ list.
  2. Apply for the loan normally — credit check, affordability, KYC.
  3. Have the work installed by an MCS-certified contractor.
  4. Submit post-installation verification (MCS certificate).
  5. The lender receives the DESNZ grant directly; your interest rate is reduced automatically.

Stacking note

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme is deducted first from the install cost; the Warm Homes Loan Scheme covers the residual, including the deposit. You can absolutely stack these.
