Chapter 4 — Warm Homes: Local Grant

The means-tested route for low-income households to get free insulation, heat pumps, solar or batteries installed at zero cost by your local council. Eligibility in plain English, with a 30-day application plan.

Action summary

If your household income is at or below £36,000 or you or someone in your household receives a qualifying benefit, apply today via gov.uk. Several councils (Birmingham, Liverpool, Warwick DC, others) are already oversubscribed for year two funding (1 April 2026 — 31 March 2027). Year three begins 1 April 2027.

What it is

A fully-funded home upgrade — insulation, a heat pump, solar, ventilation, depending on your survey. You do not pay anything back. Your local council pays the installer. The £15bn Warm Homes Plan (January 2026) allocated the funding; DESNZ published the latest scheme guidance in July 2026.

What can be funded

  • Loft and cavity wall insulation.
  • Underfloor insulation.
  • Air source or ground source heat pumps (including radiator upgrades).
  • Solar PV panels.
  • Battery storage.
  • Ventilation and draught-proofing.
  • Connecting to a heat network.

Eligibility — all of the following must be true

  1. The property is in England.
  2. The property is privately owned (by you or your private landlord; social housing has separate routes).
  3. The property has an Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) rating of D, E, F or G.
  4. One of the following:
    • Your household gross income is at or below £36,000 a year;
    • You live in an IMD Income Deciles 1–2 postcode area;
    • Someone in your household is in receipt of a qualifying means-tested benefit (Universal Credit, Pension Credit Guarantee, Income-based JSA, Income-related ESA, Housing Benefit, Child Tax Credit or Working Tax Credit with income under £36,000).

How to apply

  1. Apply online at gov.uk/apply-warm-homes-local-grant — the same form regardless of council.
  2. Call the helpline 0800 098 7950 (Mon–Fri 8am–6pm, Sat 9am–12pm) if you'd rather apply by phone or need help with documents.
  3. Your local council will contact you within 10 working days.
  4. They arrange a Retrofit Assessment (a survey) for free.
  5. They propose measures; you agree.
  6. They organise installation at zero cost to you.
  7. You receive a handover pack with warranties and manuals.

Year-two status (as of October 2026)

Multiple councils (Birmingham, Liverpool, Warwick DC, others) reported being oversubscribed for year-two funding (1 April 2026 — 31 March 2027).

Year-three applications are being taken on a "register your interest" basis today, on a waitlist rather than a confirmed allocation. Year three begins 1 April 2027.

Stacking note

Warm Homes: Local Grant can be combined with the BUS for the heat-pump portion (BUS £7,500 deducts first; the council covers the rest), and with the Warm Homes Loan Scheme for any additional cost. See Chapter 8 of the playbook for the full stacking rules.

If your council says the funding is "closed"

  1. Ask them to confirm — sometimes the published page is out of date.
  2. If confirmed, ask to register on the year-three waitlist.
  3. Submit a fresh application. Do not assume you cannot re-apply because you applied last year.
  4. If your situation has changed (new baby, illness, benefit change), update the council promptly.
  5. Some energy suppliers still accept applications to the Great British Insulation Scheme until 31 March 2026. Check with your supplier — see Chapter 2 of the playbook for the list.
