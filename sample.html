Chapter 3 — Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS) deep dive

The exact £7,500-£9,000 grant values, the four application steps, why you should never accept a "discount" from a quote, and the verified Ofgem and DESNZ sources for every number. This chapter is one of fifteen in the playbook.

Action summary

If you have an old gas, oil or LPG boiler and a quote from an MCS-certified installer, stop reading this page and ask the installer to apply for your BUS voucher today. The off-gas uplift closes on 31 March 2027. Every month you delay is money other people will take from the same fixed pot.

What it is

A government grant deducted from the installer's invoice at the point of installation. You do not receive the money. Your installer does, via Ofgem. Ofgem runs the scheme on behalf of the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ).

Who can apply

Only MCS-certified installers can apply for a BUS voucher, on your behalf. You consent via a secure link. You never fill out the BUS paperwork yourself.

Eligibility (all must be true)

  1. The property is in England or Wales.
  2. The property is residential (commercial buildings have separate rules).
  3. The installer holds a current MCS certification for the chosen technology.
  4. The property has not previously received a BUS grant at the same address.
  5. The new system is installed and commissioned, with valid paperwork.
  6. The application is properly made within voucher validity windows.

The four-step application

  1. Get three written quotes from MCS-certified installers. Verify each at mcscertified.org.uk.
  2. Ask each installer to show the BUS deduction on the quote, line by line. A good installer breaks it down as gross cost — £7,500 BUS = your invoice.
  3. Sign the Ofgem consent form via the secure link emailed to you.
  4. After install and commissioning, the installer submits the redemption and Ofgem pays them directly.

Common rejection reasons (Ofgem guidance v5.1)

  • Property has already received BUS in the past at the same address.
  • Hybrid system (heat pump + fossil fuel boiler).
  • Air-to-air heat pump in a non-residential property.
  • Installation completed outside voucher validity windows.
  • Finance arrangement means the installer or finance provider retains ownership. Conditional sale, hire purchase, consumer hire — all excluded. Standard unsecured personal loans are fine.
