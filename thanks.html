Thank you. Your order is being processed. While we verify your payment and prepare your download link, here are three things most useful to do right now:

Most urgent

Off the gas grid? Apply for the £9,000 BUS uplift before 31 March 2027.

Ask an MCS-certified installer to apply for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme with the off-gas uplift. The £9,000 rate closes 31 March 2027 in five months.

Find an MCS-certified installer

Means-tested route

EPC of D, E, F or G? Apply for Warm Homes Local Grant.

If your household income is at or below £36,000 or you receive a qualifying benefit, your local council can arrange a free retrofit upgrade.

Apply at GOV.UK

Financial shock?

Cannot afford essentials right now? Apply to your local council's Crisis and Resilience Fund.

The new Crisis and Resilience Fund (April 2026 to March 2029) is non-repayable and does not require you to be on benefits.

Apply at GOV.UK
