# GrantStack UK — what changed in 2026 A reverse-chronological log of every verified £1,000+ scheme change relevant to the UK Home Energy & Grants Stacking Playbook. This is the public audit trail that backs the "free updates for life" promise. Each entry includes the verification source and the user impact. New entries are added automatically by `AUTOMATION/scheme_change_detector.py` when a watched GOV.UK / Ofgem / DESNZ page changes, and verified by the founder before being summarised here. --- ## 16 October 2026 — playbook published **What changed**: First edition of *The UK Home Energy & Grants Stacking Playbook 2026/27* published. 15 chapters across 20 pages, covering every live scheme. **Sources used**: - [Boiler Upgrade Scheme — Ofgem property owner guidance v5 (April 2026)](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/environmental-and-social-schemes/boiler-upgrade-scheme-bus/property-owners) - [Warm Homes Plan — GOV.UK (January 2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/warm-homes-plan) - [Warm Homes: Local Grant — GOV.UK apply page](https://www.gov.uk/apply-warm-homes-local-grant) - [Crisis and Resilience Fund — GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/cost-living-help-local-council) - [MCS contractor register](https://mcscertified.org.uk/find-an-installer) - DESNZ BUS monthly statistics (August 2026) **User impact**: Buyers receive a 20-page PDF summarising every live scheme with verified sources on every number. --- ## 16 October 2026 — Warm Homes Loan Scheme consumer launch confirmed live **What changed**: A separate entry to record the lender-led Warm Homes Loan Scheme entering its initial consumer launch phase in September 2026. Initial DESNZ publication of the end-user privacy notice was 17 September 2026; the Scheme Rules confirm the **initial consumer launch phase from September 2026**, with a second lender onboarding window expected late 2026 for products launching early 2027. **Source**: [DESNZ Warm Homes Loan Scheme scheme rules (June 2026)](https://www.gov.uk). **User impact**: Homeowners wanting a heat pump, solar, battery or biomass install can now potentially borrow at a reduced rate (DESNZ pays up to 20% principal-reducing capital grant to the lender; the lender cuts the rate by up to 5 percentage points). Stackable with the BUS grant — see Chapter 5 of the playbook. --- ## 21 July 2026 — Boiler Upgrade Scheme off-gas uplift launched **What changed**: Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant for an air source or ground source heat pump is £9,000 (instead of £7,500) when the property is **off the gas grid** and the existing heating system runs on oil or LPG. The uplift runs until **31 March 2027**. **Source**: [Ofgem — Boiler Upgrade Scheme property owners](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/environmental-and-social-schemes/boiler-upgrade-scheme-bus/property-owners). **User impact**: Off-grid homeowners can claim an extra £1,500 above the standard grant. The 31 March 2027 deadline leaves roughly five months from publication date (16 October 2026) to start the application. --- ## 1 April 2026 — Crisis and Resilience Fund replaced Household Support Fund **What changed**: The Household Support Fund closed on 31 March 2026 in England. The Crisis and Resilience Fund replaced it on 1 April 2026 and runs to 31 March 2029 with ~£842m per year. It is delivered by local councils and does not require you to be on benefits. **Source**: [gov.uk/cost-living-help-local-council](https://www.gov.uk/cost-living-help-local-council). **User impact**: Low-income households in England facing a financial shock can apply to their local council for non-repayable help with energy, food, white goods or essential repairs. Wider net than the old Household Support Fund. --- ## 1 April 2026 — Warm Homes: Local Grant delivery begins **What changed**: Year one of the Warm Homes: Local Grant (the local- authority-delivered replacement for ECO4 home-energy improvements) begins delivery across eligible English councils. Year-two (April 2026 - March 2027) is now active; many councils reported being oversubscribed by October 2026, with year-three registrations opening 1 April 2027. **Source**: [DESNZ Warm Homes Local Grant guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/warm-homes-local-grant). **User impact**: Means-tested households (income ≤ £36,000 or qualifying benefit) with EPC D-G in England can get free insulation, heat pump or solar installed by their local council at zero cost. --- ## 7 January 2026 — Warm Homes Plan published **What changed**: The UK government publishes *The Warm Homes Plan*, committing £15 billion over five years to home energy upgrades. This is now the largest single UK investment in housing energy efficiency in modern history. **Source**: [Warm Homes Plan — DESNZ publications](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/warm-homes-plan). **User impact**: All other schemes listed here (BUS, Warm Homes Local Grant, Warm Homes Loan, ECO4 closure replacement) sit inside this umbrella. The plan provides the funding basis; the individual schemes are the routes.
